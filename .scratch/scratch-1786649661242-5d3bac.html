The strategy in one sentence

Business-model picture

Two tracks, different jobs

B2C is the student-value and engagement engine. B2B is the paid distribution and institution-value experiment. They share a product core, but they should not be measured or monetized as if they were the same track.

Decision gates

Two questions control further investment

The gates are commercial and behavioral. Basic data handling is an operational prerequisite, not the center of the product strategy.

Gate 1 - demand

Will an institution make a credible paid commitment?

A friendly meeting or positive feedback is not enough. Look for a named sponsor, target event/date, agreed scope, and real intent to pay.

Yes: build the minimum event-to-insight bridge.
No: keep B2B production closed; revise buyer, offer, price, or insight.
Gate 2 - value and repeat

Does the event-to-insight loop get used and earn another event?

The signal is student completion, institution use of the output without repeated hand-holding, and repeat intent - not dashboard polish.

Yes: consider scaling the channel or product.
No: revise the workflow or stop investing.
Operational prerequisite: before a live pilot, implement the basic consent, access, retention, and deletion mechanics appropriate to the agreed event flow. Keep this proportional to the pilot.

Week 1AUG 13-19

Business outcome

Make the institution proposition concrete enough to test and establish a measurable baseline for the student loop.

Product and tactics

B2C
  • Smoke-test the complete MVP journey.
  • Instrument acquisition, assessment completion, result, save, action, and return.
  • Identify obvious blockers; do not expand scope.
B2B
  • Define one pilot offer, buyer, event use case, output, and price hypothesis.
  • Prepare a student demo and one sample institution insight.
  • Book targeted institution conversations.

Evidence produced

  • Baseline B2C funnel and return metrics
  • Clear pilot offer and sample output
  • 3-5 target conversations booked or in motion
Week 2AUG 20-26

Business outcome

Test whether students get the intended payoff and whether institutions will move beyond interest toward a paid pilot.

Product and tactics

B2C
  • Run a small student test.
  • Fix the largest observed drop, confusion, or missing payoff.
  • Confirm whether users continue into a Direction or experiment.
B2B
  • Use the sample report to learn what admissions would actually use.
  • Make 3-5 explicit paid-pilot asks.
  • For a likely buyer, lock the event/date, pilot scope, institution questions, expected output, and fee.
  • Do not start B2B production code to help close the deal.
  • Capture objections by buyer, value, price, workflow, and trust.

Decision at week end

  • Top B2C bottleneck is known
  • Institution objection pattern is visible
  • Named sponsor, target event/date, agreed scope, and fee signal where demand is real
  • Commitment signed, or B2B build remains closed
Week 3AUG 27-SEP 2

If commitment is signed - build only the bridge

Business: turn the commitment into one concrete event workflow.

Product: institution event configuration and attribution; shared assessment and result; a small institution question set; event-scoped sharing and submission storage; signed or rotatable read-only report access; aggregate view, student list, record view, CSV, and minimal funnel analytics.

Evidence: one synthetic student can complete the full event-to-report flow.

If no commitment - keep the gate closed

Business: test whether the blocker is buyer, offer, price, insight, or workflow.

Product: no B2B production code. Improve the largest measured B2C bottleneck and update only demo or sales artifacts tied to a concrete objection.

Evidence: one clear objection pattern and a revised hypothesis.

Week 4SEP 3-9

If commitment is signed - run the full loop

Business: see whether the sponsor can use the output and wants another event.

Product: dry run or live pilot; measure event entry, result, shared submission, and B2C continuation separately; verify report and CSV use; run critical scoring/export checks plus browser and device smoke tests; use a short event runbook; fix pilot blockers only.

Evidence: students complete the flow, the institution uses the output without repeated hand-holding, and repeat intent is explicit.

If no commitment - make a strategy decision

Business: narrow the institution segment, change the offer or price, continue selling with a revised hypothesis, or pause B2B.

Product: continue B2C loop improvement based on measured evidence. Do not invent a B2B backlog.

Evidence: an explicit next-cycle decision rather than sunk-cost engineering.

Scope wall

What belongs - and what does not

A feature belongs only if it strengthens student acquisition, student value and return, or institution willingness to pay and use the result.

Do now

  • B2C MVP journey and return loop
  • Acquisition, activation, action, and return instrumentation
  • Small student tests and biggest-friction fixes
  • B2B pilot offer, pitches, demo, and sample insight
  • For serious demand, lock sponsor, event/date, scope, expected output, and price signal
  • Shared-core fixes required by both tracks

Build only after paid commitment

  • Institution event configuration and QR/event-link attribution
  • Mobile-web event entry with no required app install or account
  • Small institution question set
  • Event-scoped sharing and submission storage
  • Signed or rotatable read-only report access
  • Aggregate view, student list, record view, and CSV
  • Minimal event funnel metrics and critical-path tests
  • Browser/device smoke tests and a short event runbook

Not in this four-week cycle

  • Mentor calls or mentor marketplace
  • Paid B2C tiers or payment infrastructure
  • Content feed or broad gamification
  • Full B2B dashboard, accounts, or roles
  • Real-time reporting infrastructure
  • CMS, question builder, or institution self-service
  • CRM or enrollment integrations, campaign tooling, and notifications
  • Program or major mapping, fit scoring, recommendation engines, and platform infrastructure
  • Unrelated B2C feature expansion outside the measured value loop
